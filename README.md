# 🩺 VitalQR — Emergency Medical ID

**When every second matters, the right medical information matters.**

VitalQR is a digital emergency medical identification platform designed to make essential medical information accessible when a person cannot communicate during an emergency. It uses a scannable QR code to help people access a medical summary and emergency contact details.

## 🌟 About the Project

During a medical emergency, first responders and bystanders may not know a person's blood group, allergies, existing medical conditions, or emergency contacts. VitalQR aims to make these essential details easier to access through a medical profile linked to an emergency QR code.

A key feature is the **offline medical summary** embedded in the QR code, allowing the included information to be read by a compatible scanner without requiring an internet connection.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Digital Medical Profile** — Organize essential personal and medical information.
- **Emergency QR Code** — Make the configured medical summary accessible through QR scanning.
- **Offline Accessibility** — Include essential information directly in the QR code for offline scanning.
- **Emergency Contacts** — Keep important contact details available for emergencies.
- **Medical Information Management** — Maintain medical details in one place.
- **Database Integration** — Use Supabase to support authentication and data storage.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Create your medical profile.
2. Enter your medical information and emergency contact details.
3. Review and confirm the information.
4. Generate your emergency QR code.
5. Scan the QR code to access the information included in the medical summary.

## 🎯 Project Goal

The goal of VitalQR is to explore how accessible technology can help people communicate critical medical information during emergencies and potentially reduce delays in identifying important medical details.

## 🔐 Privacy and Safety

VitalQR is a medical identification project, not a replacement for professional medical care. Information embedded in a QR code may be visible to anyone who scans it. Only include information intended for emergency access, and ensure appropriate database security and access controls are configured.
